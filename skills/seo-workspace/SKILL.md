# Workspace panel

Call `open_workspace`. In clients that show MCP Apps, the panel opens beside the chat with four tabs: Listings (SEO editor), New listing (photo upload), History (revert) and Account.

- A click on "Apply to Etsy" or "Revert" in the panel is the seller's approval for that one change.
- New listings are not published from the panel: it uploads photos and asks you in the chat to create the listing. Follow [create a listing](../create-etsy-listing/SKILL.md) from there.
- If the client cannot show the panel, every task works with the other tools in the chat.
