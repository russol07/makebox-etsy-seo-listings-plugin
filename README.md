# SEO Etsy by MakeBox AI

Connect Claude to your Etsy shop to improve listing SEO and create new listings. The plugin adds a listing assistant, four guided skills and the connection to `https://app.makebox.ai/seo/mcp`.

Built for Etsy sellers who want better Etsy SEO without copy-pasting: an Etsy listing assistant that writes Etsy titles, all 13 Etsy tags and listing descriptions from your product facts, checks them against Etsy's character and tag rules, and creates new Etsy listings with category, attributes, shipping and processing profiles, photos and personalization.

## What you can do

- Improve one listing at a time: title, 13 tags, description, materials and styles, written from your real product facts.
- Check copy against Etsy's limits and catch tags that contradict the product or claims nobody stated.
- See the before/after and apply it to Etsy only after you approve. Revert any change within 30 days.
- Create a new physical listing: category, attributes, price, quantity, shipping and processing profiles, photos, personalization. Save it as an Etsy draft or publish it.
- Open a visual panel beside the chat (in clients that support MCP Apps) to edit, apply and revert with your own click.

Nothing reaches Etsy without your approval.

## Without a connection

The skills still help: paste a listing and get copy-ready title, tags and description, checked against Etsy's rules. Nothing is saved to Etsy.

## Connect

1. Install the plugin and connect **SEO Etsy by MakeBox AI**. Sign in with a MakeBox account (password or Google) and accept the terms.
2. Ask Claude to connect your Etsy shop. Open the link it gives you and approve access on Etsy.
3. Ask, for example: "Improve the SEO of my best-selling listing", "Check the tags of my neon sign listing", "Create a listing for this mug from these photos", or "Open the SEO workspace".
